MASHINA performance features

Six MIDI-routed tracks

Each track has its own MIDI receive channel, volume and pan. Share a channel to layer sounds or use separate channels for different controllers.

Keyboard zones and tuning

Split or layer instruments by note or velocity, transpose tracks up to three octaves and adjust fine tuning independently.

Additive live timing for every engine

Delay each track by 0–50 ms with GLO → MIX → TUNING K3, including melodic engines. On the four drum engines, SEQ → MIDI MAP K4 adds 0–50 ms per pad. The delays add together and are saved with projects.

\n

Additional melodic synthesizers

ATMOS for evolving pads, BRASS for synthetic wind sounds and INDUSTRIAL for heavy basses and leads, alongside the existing JIANT engines.

Four 12-sound drum engines

DRUM-X expands the original kit; DRUM-LAB adds electronic percussion, DRUM-AC acoustic-style drums, and DRUM-IND metallic and mechanical textures.

Factory presets inspired by history

32 USER and 96 replaceable LIBRARY positions are available on FM1. The Web Library includes 107 complete factory sounds. Many presets are named after philosophers and historical thinkers; DRUM-X, DRUM-LAB, DRUM-AC and DRUM-IND use numbered BUTTLER, ZIZEK, KRIPKE and BADIOU kits.

Live MIDI control

32 MIDI CC macros, MIDI Learn and live commands for adjusting and varying sounds from an external controller.

Mixer, EQ and performance interface

Six-track live mixer, shared three-band EQ, streamlined orange-and-gray controls and a quick panic function.

Projects and live recall

Eight projects with four A–D sections share numbered sound references and retain independent performance settings.

Before flashing

Installing from another firmware or incompatible older MASHINA version clears saved sounds, projects and settings automatically. Save anything you want to keep first. Compatible updates retain data.
