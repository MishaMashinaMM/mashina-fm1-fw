Performance features

Six MIDI-routed tracks

Each track has its own MIDI receive channel, volume and pan. Share a channel to layer sounds or use separate channels for different controllers.

Keyboard zones and tuning

Split or layer instruments by MIDI note or velocity. Transpose each melodic track ±36 semitones and fine-tune ±50 cents.

Melodic synthesizers

ATMOS creates evolving pads, BRASS morphs synthetic winds, INDUSTRIAL creates heavy basses and leads. Other synthesis engines are available too.

Two 12-sound drum engines

DRUM-X and DRUM-LAB offer individually editable sounds, customizable MIDI mappings, velocity response and choke behavior.

32 MIDI CC macros

Learn incoming controller CCs and map them to live sound parameters, with continuous ranges, toggles, reverse and bypass.

Effects, EQ and live projects

Use shared three-band EQ, effects, project save and recall, and panic mode in a fast, animation-free interface.

Before flashing

Back up projects, user presets and settings. Keep a recovery method available when switching from other firmware.
