What’s in v0.9

v0.8: KEYBOARD ZONES

GLO → MIX → ZONES is a second six-row mixer-style screen. K1 MIN and K2 MAX define note range; K3 V.MIN and K4 V.MAX define velocity range. Each of six tracks stores its own settings in projects. Same-channel instruments can split or overlap across note and velocity zones with external MIDI. The FM1 built-in keybed still targets the selected track.

NEW: BRASS — SAX to TRUMPET

Expressive, synthetic wind-instrument engine with a continuous 0–127 SAX/TRUMPET morph, pressure, buzz, breath, formant/body shaping and growl. EDIT 3 offers optional FAT, GRIND, METAL and CRUSH. Six factory presets; four-voice engine cap.

NEW: INDUSTRIAL — heavy bass and leads

Six aggressive presets, dual oscillators, FAT detune, GRIND, METAL, CRUSH and FEED, plus independent WAVE2/MIX/SUB/MODE on EDIT 3. Synth sounds can be combined across MIDI-routed tracks.

Two independent 12-pad drum engines

DRUM-X retains the original eight sounds and adds independent B2, S2, O2 and T2 instances. DRUM-LAB provides 12 editable synthesized pads and kit-wide shaping controls.

Live Recall and project management

Long-press REC to toggle Live Recall. Then tap FX, SEL, ENV or LFO to recall project slots 1–4. SAVE → LIBRARY includes CLEAR SLOT and NEW PROJECT; saved-section indicators were corrected and device-tested.

NEW v0.9: TUNING — per-track pitch and fine detune

GLO → MIX → TUNING is the third six-row page, after MIXER and ZONES. K1 sets TRN −36…+36 semitones (three octaves); K2 sets FINE −50…+50 cents for fuller, slightly detuned layers. K3/K4 are reserved. Synth pitch changes without changing MIDI note ownership; drums keep their mapped pitches. The Korg X5D was used to verify the feature on FM1 hardware.

Six-track MIDI routing and mixer

GLO opens directly to MIX → MIXER. Set each track's RX channel (K1), level (K2), pan (K3); K4 is now unused. Per-track transpose moved to TUNING. Settings persist per project; PRESETS adjusts the master DJ Filter only on MIXER. CC88–90 can select temporary live RX channels for T1–T3.

32 CC macros and MIDI Learn

Up to 32 saved CC macros per project, with range/toggle, reverse and independent enable settings. MIDI Learn provides 32 additional parameter destinations and CC fan-out across tracks.

Shared three-band EQ

Global EQ before effects: LOW 50 Hz, MID 700 Hz and HIGH 10 kHz, each adjustable ±15 dB. Its settings are saved with each project.

Built for live performance

Eight shared synthesis voices, the original melodic engines and effects, and a live-only layout: SEQ opens DRUM MIDI MAP, PLAY opens DRUM PERFORMANCE, and REC opens MIDI CC MACROS. The sequencer is disabled.

v0.9 orange UI successfully flashed and device-tested on FM1 (November 18, 2026).

Keyboard Zones and TUNING (TRN ±36 semitones, FINE ±50 cents) were tested with a Korg X5D, and the new orange/gray live interface was subsequently flashed to a physical FM1 and reported working correctly. This is owner-reported functional testing, not exhaustive endurance or recovery certification. Back up projects/presets before flashing and keep a working recovery installer. Older firmware may not understand projects saved with nonzero fine tuning.

For MASHINA controls, see the v0.9 User Manual and current README. The interactive upstream JIANT manual is inherited.
